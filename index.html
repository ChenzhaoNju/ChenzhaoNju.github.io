I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Nanjing University, advised by Prof. Ying Tai. I work in PCALab, led by Prof. Jian Yang.

My research focuses on visual generation, image restoration, and multimodal understanding. I am particularly interested in high-resolution image and video synthesis, frequency-aware modeling, and audiovisual captioning.

Research Interests

I study how to generate and recover visual content with faithful structure and fine detail. My work on high-resolution generation explores latent cascades, frequency-aware training, and high-quality datasets for image and video synthesis. In image restoration, I use spectral representations and generative priors to improve detail recovery at ultra-high resolutions.

I also work on audiovisual understanding, with a focus on aligning sound, visual content, and events over time. This connects my interests in multimodal captioning with the data pipelines that support video foundation models.

Meituan · Video Generation & Multimodal Pretraining
